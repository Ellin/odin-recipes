Vegan Mushroom Pasta

Description

A creamy mushroom pasta - but vegan with oat cream!

Ingredients

  • 500g pasta
  • 750mL vegan oat cream
  • 1/2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp vegan margarine
  • 5 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 8 large white button mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 tsp coarse black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • Seasoning

Steps

  1. Boil pasta in salted water, drain, and mix in the margarine. Set aside.
  2. Cook the garlic, mushrooms, and onions in a separate pan with vegetable oil.
  3. Once the mushrooms and vegetables are cooked, add the oat cream, salt, and pepper into the same pan. Stir.
  4. Bring the cream sauce to a boil and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Mix the cream sauce with the pasta.
