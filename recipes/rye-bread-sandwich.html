Rye Bread Sandwich

Description

My simple go-to rye bread sandwich.

Ingredients

  • 1 rye bread round, sliced in half
  • Fried egg
    • 1 egg
    • Margarine
    • Salt
  • 2 slices of deli meat
  • Broccoli sprouts
  • Tomato
  • Cucumber
  • Mayo

Steps

  1. Mix and fry 1 egg on a pan coated with margarine. Add a light pinch of salt.
  2. Toast the rye bread.
  3. Slice tomato and cucumber into rounds.
  4. Spread mayo on the toasted bread.
  5. Layer the egg, deli meat, and vegetables between the bread slices.
  6. Enjoy your rye bread sandwich!
