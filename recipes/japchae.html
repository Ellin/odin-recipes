Japchae

Description

Japchae is a traditional Korean noodle dish. It's often served as a side dish, but it also makes a delicious main dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 package (14.1oz) sweet potato noodles
  • 1 bunch - spinach
  • 5 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 15 shiitake mushrooms, rehydrate & sliced
  • 1 large carrot, peeled & julienned
  • 1 medium yellow onion, sliced
  • 4 green onions, chopped
  • Seasoning
    • 10 tbs soy sauce
    • 4tbs sugar
    • 2.5tbs sesame oil
    • 1/2tsp salt
    • 3/8tsp fine ground black pepper
  • Sesame seeds for garnish

Steps

  1. Boil noodles.
  2. Rinse cooked noodles under cold water. Drain and set aside.
  3. Cook all vegetables in a wok.
  4. Add the noodles and seasoning.
  5. Cook until hot.
  6. Serve and garnish with sesame seeds.
